2. QORAALKA DHAMMAYSTIRAN EE SHIRARKA, PROPOSAL-KA & BANDHIGYADA DUGSIYADA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

UGU HORREYN: MAXAY TAHAY SABABTA UU DUGSIGAAGU U BAAHAN YAHAY APPKAN?
Maamulka dugsigu waa shaqo baaxad weyn oo u baahan xisaab-tanag, saxnaan, iyo 
xog-sugnaan. App-kani waa nidaam dhammaystiran (All-In-One School Management System) 
oo loogu talagalay inuu daboolo baahiyaha dugsiyada hoose, dhexe iyo sare, isagoo 
u fududeynaya maamulka, macallimiinta, khasnajiga, iyo waalidiinta.


QAYBAHA & SHAQOOYINKA CAUDDADA AH EE APPKU KA KOOBAN YAHAY:

1️⃣ QAYBTA GUUD EE DASHBOARD-KA & SHIFT-YADA (Real-Time Control):
   - Nidaamka 3-da Shift: Hal badhan ku kala dooro Subax, Galab, ama Habeen si aad
     u kala aragto xogta ardayda shift kasta.
   - Tirakoob toos ah: Tirada ardayda firfircoon, fasallada, macallimiinta, iyo 
     boqolkiiba inta maanta joogtay ama maqnayd.
   - Warbixin Maaliyadeed oo Kooban: Lacagta bishan soo gashay iyo inta baaqi ku ah.

2️⃣ DIIWAANGELINTA & MAAMULKA ARDAYDA (Student Records):
   - Xogta shakhsiga ah, sawirka ardayga, lambarka aqoonsiga (Roll Number), taleefanka 
     waalidka, iyo taariikhda qorista.
   - Nidaamka "Recycle Bin": Haddii arday si khalad ah loo tirtiro, marnaba xogtiisu 
     ma lumayso; waxaad ku celin kartaa hal taabasho (Restore) ama si rasmi ah u 
     tirtiri kartaa.

3️⃣ JOOGITAANKA MAALINLAHA AH (Smart Attendance Tracker):
   - Qabashada joogitaanka fasal kasta ilbidhiqsiyo gudahood.
   - Badhanka "Dhammaan Jooga" oo macallinka u badbaadinaya waqti badan.
   - Aqoonsiga ardayda maqan, daahay, ama fasaxan.
   - Fariin toos ah oo SMS ama WhatsApp loogu diri karo waalidka ardayga maqan.

4️⃣ NIDAAMKA IMTIXAANAADKA & NATIIJOOYINKA (Examination & Marksheet):
   - Galinta dhibcaha maado kasta (Xisaab, Af-Soomaali, Carabi, English, Saynis, iwm).
   - Xisaabin toos ah (Automated Calculation): Total-ka, Boqolkiiba (%), Darajada 
     (A, B, C, D, F), iyo in ardaygu "GUDUBAY" ama "DHACAY".
   - Kala-sarraynta Fasalka (Class Rank): App-ku isagaa si toos ah u ogaanaya 
     ardayga 1-aad galay, 2-aad, 3-aad ilaa kan ugu dambeeya.

5️⃣ MAAMULKA LACAGAHA & RASMYADA (Fees & Invoicing):
   - Qaadashada fiiga bilaha ah, diiwaangelinta, iyo fiiga imtixaanka.
   - Taageerada dhammaan hababka lacag-bixinta casriga ah (EVC Plus, Zaad, eDahab, Cash).
   - Rasiidh rasmi ah oo la daabici karo ama PDF ahaan loogu diri karo waalidka.
   - Ogaanshaha baaqiga arday kasta ku hadhsan si looga fogaado khasaare maaliyadeed.

6️⃣ WARQADDA CADDEYNTA & FASAXA IMTIXAANKA (Student Clearance):
   - Xaqiijinta in ardaygu lacagta dhameeyay ka hor inta aanu gelin imtixaanka.
   - Daabacaadda kaadhadhka imtixaanka (Exam Clearance Passes) ee fasalka oo dhan hal mar.

7️⃣ XARUNTA DAABACAADDA & WARBIXINADA (Professional PDF & Printing):
   - Daabacaad tayo sare leh oo cabbirka rasmiga ah ee A4 ah:
     * Warbixinta Guud ee Dugsiga
     * Kaadhka Natiijada Shakhsiga ah (Student Report Card) oo wata calaamadda dugsiga
     * Natiijada Guud ee Fasalka (Master Marksheet)
     * Liiska Magacyada Fasalka iyo Taleefannada Waalidiinta
   - Looma baahna internet si wax loo daabaco; waxay si toos ah ugu xidhantaa printer-ka.

8️⃣ PORTAL-KA ARDAYDA & WAALIDIINTA (Self-Service Portal):
   - Waalidku ama ardaygu wuxuu si sahlan u eegi karaa natiijada imtixaanka, 
     joogitaanka, iyo haddii fiigii la bixiyey isagoo isticmaalaya ID-ga ardayga.

9️⃣ AMNIGA & KALA SOOCA AWOODAHA (Role-Based Access Control):
   - Furaha sirta ah ee lagu galo nidaamka.
   - Qaybinta awoodaha:
     * Maamule / Principal: Awood buuxda.
     * Macallin: Galinta dhibcaha iyo joogitaanka fasaladiisa oo kaliya.
     * Khasnaji: Maamulka lacagaha iyo rasiidhada oo kaliya.

🔟 KAYDINTA CLOUD-KA & GOOGLE DRIVE (Zero Data Loss Guarantee):
   - Xogtaadu waxay ku kaydsan tahay taleefankaaga xitaa haddii internetku go'an yahay.
   - Hal taabasho ku kaydi (Backup) Google Drive-kaaga gaarka ah si aysan xogtu kuugu 
     lumin haddii taleefanku kaa lumo ama kaa xumaado.
   - Soo celin degdeg ah (Restore) marka aad taleefan cusub ku shubato.
