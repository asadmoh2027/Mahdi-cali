1. GUUDMAR & UJEEDDADA NIDAAMKA (SYSTEM OVERVIEW)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Magaca App-ka: Mahdi Cali School Management System
Madasha (Platform): Native Android (Kotlin & Jetpack Compose)
Nooca Nidaamka: Offline-First, Secure, Enterprise School ERP & Student Portal
Qaybaha Isticmaalayaasha:
  1. Maamulaha Dugsiga (Principal / Admin) - Awood buuxda oo maamul.
  2. Super Admin (System Owner / IT) - Maamulka heer sare ee xisaabaadka.
  3. Macallimiinta (Teachers) - Diiwaangelinta xaadirinta & buundooyinka fasallada.
  4. Khasnajiga (Cashier) - Maamulka lacag-bixinta ardayda & rasiidhada.
  5. Ardayda & Waalidiinta (Portal) - Baadhitaanka natiijada & kaadhadhka warbixinta.


2. QAAB-DHISMEEDKA FARSAMO & TIGNOOLAJIYADA (ARCHITECTURE & TECH STACK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Hannaanka Software-ka: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel) + Repository Pattern + Clean Data Layer.
- Luuqadda: Kotlin 100%.
- UI Framework: Jetpack Compose oo ku salaysan Material Design 3 (M3).
- Keydinta Gudaha (Local DB): Room Database (SQLite) oo leh B-Tree Performance Indexes
  (idx_students_classId, idx_attendance_studentId, idx_exam_marks_examId, iwm.)
  si xogtu u noqoto mid aad u dheeraysa xataa marka ardaydu gaadhaan kumanaan.
- Nidaamka Soft-Delete: Xogta lama tirtiro si toos ah, balse waxaa loo diraa
  Recycle Bin (Qashin-qubka) oo dib looga soo celin karo.
- Keydinta Daruuraha (Cloud Storage):
  1. Google Drive Web App Script: Hal-taabasho oo xogta oo dhan JSON loogu shubo
     ama looga soo dejiyo Google Drive.
  2. Cloud Auto-Sync: Is-waafajinta xogta xilliga dhabta ah marka internet jiro.
- Nidaamka Daabacaadda & PDF (Print Engine):
  Android Print Spooler oo leh PrintDocumentAdapter wrapper ilaalinaya xusuusta
  (Memory Leak-free & GC-protected), kaas oo taageera A4 Format, kala-jebinta
  bogagga (page-break), astaanta dugsiga oo optimized ah (120x120px), Serial Code,
  iyo shaambadaha rasmiga ah.


3. QAYBAHA & SHAQOOYINKA MUHIIMKA AH (CORE MODULES & FEATURES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. XAKAMAYNTA DOORKA & GELITAANKA (AUTHENTICATION & RBAC)
  - Maamulidda akoonnada: Administrator, Teacher, Cashier, Super Admin.
  - Xannibaadda awoodaha (Permission Gates): Macallimiintu ma tirtiri karaan
    fasallo ama arday, mana abuuri karaan khidmado cusub.
  - Akoonno qarsoon iyo qufulka xisaabaadka shaqaalaha shaqada ka tagay.

B. MAAMULKA ARDAYDA (STUDENT MANAGEMENT)
  - Soo-saarista tooska ah ee ID-ga gaarka ah (tusaale: AUTO-001, AUTO-002).
  - Diiwaangelinta: Magaca, Hooyada, Taleefanka waalidka, Fasalka, Geliinka.
  - Calaamadaynta ardayda deeqda waxbarasho haysata (Scholarship / Free).
  - Shaandhaynta tooska ah (Search by Name, Roll ID, ama Class).
  - Tirtiridda wadajirka ah (Bulk Delete) ee fasalka oo dhan hal mar oo maamuluhu samayn karo.

C. FASALADA & GELIINNADA (CLASSES & SHIFTS)
  - Kala saaridda geliinnada: Gelin Hore (Morning), Gelin Danbe (Afternoon), Dhammaan (All).
  - Xilsaarka macallinka fasalka (Incharge Teacher).
  - Tirakoobka agabka dugsiga: Fasalada, Kuraasta, Musqulaha, Xafiisyada, iyo Jikada Cuntada.

D. XAADIRINTA & DABAGALKA WAKHTIGA (ATTENDANCE & AUDIT)
  - 4 Xaaladood oo xaadirin ah: Jooga (Present), Maqan (Absent), Habsan (Late), Fasax (Free).
  - Transparency & Audit Tracker: Diiwaangelinta saacadda saxda ah ee macallinku
    xaadiriyay iyo in ay ku jirtay saacadaha shaqada (08:00-12:00 / 14:00-16:30) ama ka baxsanayd.
  - Warqadda Digniinta Habsanka (Student Late Warning Slip) oo PDF loo daabici karo.

E. IMTIXAANNADA & 7-DA MAADDO EE RASMIGA AH (EXAMS & MARKS)
  - 7-da Maaddo ee heerka dugsiyada:
      1. Diinta Islaamka
      2. Af-Soomaali
      3. Xisaab
      4. Saynis
      5. Cilmiga Bulshada
      6. English
      7. Carabi
  - Xisaabinta Buundooyinka: Teeramka 1aad (Max: 50) + Teeramka 2aad (Max: 50) = Wadarta (/100).
  - Xisaabinta tooska ah ee Celceliska, Darajada (A, B, C, D, F) iyo Xaaladda (Baasay / Dhacay).
  - Mark Change Audit Trail: Diiwaanka qofkii buundo beddelay, tii hore, tii cusub, iyo sababta.

F. MAAMULKA LACAGAHA (FEE MANAGEMENT)
  - Lacagaha lagu qaabilo: USD iyo SLS (Somali Shilling).
  - Dabagalka lacagaha dhiman (Pending Fees) iyo lacagaha la bixiyay (Paid Fees).
  - Rasiidho la daabacan karo iyo dhoofinta xogta ee Excel/CSV.

G. BARTA ARDAYDA & WAALIDIINTA (PUBLIC STUDENT PORTAL)
  - Shaashad u gaar ah ardayda iyo waalidiinta iyagoon u baahnayn login shaqaale.
  - Gelinta Student ID-ga oo kaliya si loo helo Report Card dhammaystiran,
    xaadirinta boqolkiiba, iyo buundooyinka maaddo kasta.


4. WARQADAHA & DAABACAADDA RASMIGA AH (OFFICIAL PDF PRINTING ENGINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
App-ku wuxuu si buuxda u daabacaa una dhoofiyaa PDF-yo rasmi ah oo qaab A4 ah:
  1. Warqadda Ardayga (Official Clearance & Leaving Record Sheet):
     - Dugsiga hore, dugsiga cusub, fasalka uu u gudbayo, iyo saxeexyada kormeeraha & maamulaha.
  2. Marksheet-ka Ardayga (Annual Marksheet & Results Card):
     - Qof keliya (Single PDF) ama Dhammaan Fasalka oo Dhan hal mar (All Class PDF)
       oo bogag kala madax-bannaan leh (page-break: always).
  3. Warbixinta Guud ee Dugsiga (Comprehensive School Overview Report):
     - Tirakoobka ardayda, Garaafka Wiilasha & Gabdhaha (Gender Percentage Bar Chart),
       Tirada Macallimiinta (Gelin Hore & Danbe), Agabka Dugsiga, iyo shaxda fasal-kasta.
  4. Warbixinta Xaadirinta & Saacadaha Macallimiinta (Monthly Attendance & Audit Sheet).
  5. Xaashida Natiijooyinka Imtixaanka Fasalka (Class Exam Results Roster).


5. NAQSHADDA & MIDABADA (UI/UX DESIGN SYSTEM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Design Language: Material Design 3 (M3).
- Midabada Astaanta u ah (Color Scheme):
  * Teal Primary: #006A6B (Midabka koowaad ee dugsiga)
  * Teal Dark: #004D4E (Ciwaannada waaweyn)
  * Pass Green: #15803D (Baasay / Xaadir)
  * Fail Red: #C62828 (Dhacay / Maqan / Tirtirid)
  * Warning Amber: #B45309 (Habsan / Lacag dhiman)
  * Male Stat Blue: #0284C7
  * Female Stat Pink: #D946EF
- UX & Accessibility:
  * Minimum Touch Target: 48dp x 48dp dhammaan badhannada.
  * LazyColumn Keys: Liisasku dib iskuma sawiraan xilliga rogidda (60fps fluid scroll).
  * Edge-to-Edge display oo taageeraya dhammaan dhererrada taleefannada.


6. AMNIGA, FASAXYADA & BADBAADADA XOGTA (SECURITY & POLICY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Fasaxyada: Kaliya INTERNET iyo ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE ayaa loo baahan yahay.
  App-ku ma weydiisto isticmaalaha wax fasaxyo halis ah (No dangerous permissions).
- Dhoofinta Faylasha: Waxaa loo isticmaalaa Android FileProvider oo 100% waafaqsan
  xeerarka Google Play Developer Program.
- Badbaado 100%: Xogta dugsigu waxay leedahay keyd saddex-geesood ah:
  Room SQLite (Local) + Recycle Bin (Recovery) + Google Drive Cloud Sync.
